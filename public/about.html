The Story Behind the Tool

This converter started as a weekend project when I realized I needed to convert "cups" to "milliliters" for a recipe and was too lazy to remember the conversion factors.

It evolved into something I use almost daily. Whether you're cooking, working on DIY projects, or just settling a debate with friends about how many teaspoons are in a gallon, I hope you find it as useful as I do.

Thanks for visiting! I hope this app makes your life at least 2.54 centimeters easier (that's exactly one inch, by the way—see how handy this knowledge is?).
